The tree visualises the probability of demographic direction only. Age tendency — children, working-age or ageing — acts as an additional conditional probability within each branch and further differentiates the demographic outcome sampled in each period.

Probability basis. EUROPOP2025 regional frequencies anchor the Period 01 Growth / Decline weights (28.2% / 71.8% before introducing the modelled Stable band); age-profile tendencies are informed by Idescat population projections, base 2024. These are modelling weights rather than official probabilities for Barcelona. Within each 25-year period, the joint demographic probabilities are converted into annual frequencies and then shuffled using the stochastic seed: a 20% outcome therefore appears approximately five times in a 25-year block, but its position within that block varies.
