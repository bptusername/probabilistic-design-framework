T3 → T4 → … → TnThe same probabilistic update can be repeated for as many successive states as required, redefining the external variable at each transition.

The trajectory is path-dependent: the realised condition of each shingle at one state determines the transition probabilities available at the next. Moisture exposure can be redefined independently for every transition, while weathered shingles remain terminal.
