Probabilistic transformation

How to read the model
1 · Sample demographic future
One demographic outcome is selected according to the assigned probability distribution.
2 · Translate into spatial demand
Its population change and demographic profile determine the amount and distribution of program demand.
The probabilistic component operates at the level of demographic change; spatial allocation is deterministic once the demographic outcome has been sampled.
